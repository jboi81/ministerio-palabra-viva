# Ministerio Palabra Viva SC — Website

I built this website for my church, Ministerio Palabra Viva, a Spanish-language church in Greenville, SC. It's a redesign of the church's web presence that puts the livestream, sermons, and worship music right on the main page instead of sending people off-site to find them.

**Live site:** https://ministerio-palabra-viva.netlify.app

## What the site does

- **En Vivo** player that shows the live service during service times (Sundays 10:00 AM, Tuesdays 8:30 PM) and replays the most recent service the rest of the week
- Sermons & Bible studies section, with a featured study and the church's study series
- **Alabanza y adoración** music player that cycles through the church's worship playlist from YouTube
- Service times, kids & youth ministry info, church history, and a plan-your-visit section with map and directions
- Download buttons for the church's official app (App Store & Google Play)
- The church's full menu, including a full-screen menu on mobile
- Design follows the church's own black, white, and gold branding and uses their real logo

## Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework. Hosted on Netlify. All video is embedded from the church's YouTube channel.
